Sookshm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Sookshm ("the app") compresses and resizes photos, signatures and PDFs on your phone.

Your files stay on your device

All processing happens on your device. The app has no accounts and no backend, and it never uploads your photos, signatures, PDFs or any other file. Temporary working copies are kept in the app's private storage and deleted when a job ends.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo inside the app. Pictures are not sent anywhere.
  • Photos and files: you pick files with the Android system picker. The app does not request broad storage or media access.
  • When saving, location data (EXIF GPS) is removed from the output by default.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows ads in the free version. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and approximate usage data to serve ads. See Google's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat handles premium purchases. It receives purchase and subscription information and an anonymous app user ID. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Payments are processed by Google Play. The app never sees your payment details.

The app has no analytics SDK of its own.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will be published at this location.
